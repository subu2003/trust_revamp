About Us

Our mission

Uma Lakshmi Educational Trust exists so that no deserving child in Tamil Nadu has to give up education for want of money. We identify meritorious students from economically disadvantaged families, pay their school and college fees directly to their institutions, and stand beside them until they reach their goals.

We believe that giving basic education to many children matters more than giving extra education to a few; that educating a girl child educates a whole family; that no child who loses a parent should have to stop studying; and that donors deserve clear, accurate and honest information about where every rupee goes.

The verse that inspired the trust

Mahakavi Bharathiyar

Bharathiyar wrote that one may build a thousand feeding houses and establish ten thousand temples, and that all such acts of charity bring merit — yet greater than all of them put together is the merit of teaching a single poor child to read.

It was this verse that moved our founder to establish an educational trust in his wife's name. It remains the measure of everything we do: not how much we give, but how many children we help to learn.

Our values

What guides every decision we make.

Compassion

Every decision begins with the child in front of us, and the love in whose memory the trust was founded.

Merit with need

We support students who have both the ability to learn and a real financial need — without discrimination of any kind.

Transparency

Fees go directly to institutions, accounts are audited every year, and donors can see exactly what their gift achieved.

Dignity

Students are partners, not beneficiaries. Their information is kept private and their achievements are celebrated.

Continuity

The trust is built around a purpose, not a person, so that the chain of kindness continues beyond generations.

Our policies

How we work.

Clear rules, applied the same way to every applicant, every year. These are the policies the trustees follow when selecting and supporting students.

01Who can applyStudents of government and government-aided schools and colleges in Tamil Nadu whose family income is limited, with a satisfactory academic record. Preference is given to girl children, to students who have lost a parent, and to first-generation learners.
02How students are selectedApplications are reviewed by the trustees on both need and merit, using the application form, mark sheets, income details and the headmaster's or principal's attestation. Selection is made without regard to religion, caste, community or gender.
03How fees are paidSponsorship is paid by cheque or transfer in favour of the institution, never in cash to individuals. Cheques are presented at the annual function or posted to outstation students for handing over to their school or college.
04Continuing supportStudents may apply again each year. Continued support depends on regular attendance, passing all subjects and the institution's confirmation that the student is enrolled.
05Privacy and accountabilityApplicant and family information is used only for selection and is never published or shared. The trust's accounts are audited annually and its registrations under Sections 12A and 80G are maintained in good standing.
