In loving memory

Uma Lakshmi

“To spread her compassion towards educating poor children, and share her love with future generations.”

Uma Lakshmi Educational Trust was founded in Chennai on 11 June 2022 by SP. Nachiappan in memory of his beloved wife. What began as a tribute has grown into a five-year commitment: every year the trust selects deserving students from government and government-aided schools and colleges, and pays their fees directly to the institution.

A kindness done in one name now lives on in the lives of 265 students.

SP. NachiappanFounder & Managing Trustee
Founder's message

A commitment beyond generations.

Education is not merely the acquisition of knowledge; it is the foundation upon which individuals, families and society build a better future. Uma Lakshmi Educational Trust was established to ensure that no deserving child is deprived of education because of financial difficulties.

We firmly believe that every child deserves an opportunity, every talent deserves encouragement, and every dream deserves a chance to become reality. This trust is not built around an individual; it is built around a purpose.

Our solemn commitment To serve with sincerity, transparency and accountability · to support deserving students without discrimination · to recognise genuine achievement · to stand beside students as they progress towards their ambitions · to reach more children every year.

Our commitment is to education. Our purpose is to empower. Our legacy is to transform lives.

Recognised by the Income Tax Department

Permanently registered under Section 12A and approved under Section 80G.

Donations to Uma Lakshmi Educational Trust are eligible for income-tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961. Every donor receives a receipt bearing the trust's registration details.
